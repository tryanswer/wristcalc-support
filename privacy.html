腕上计算

生效日期：2026年10月14日

腕上计算免费、离线使用，无需账号。应用不含广告、分析 SDK、开发者后台服务或网络权限，不向开发者传送计算内容。

表达式及最多20条成功结果保存在手表的应用本地存储中，用于显示、复用和删除。应用不申请位置、通讯录、健康、相机或麦克风权限。应用内没有数据采集同意门槛。

你可以在“最近”删除记录。卸载会移除应用的活动本地存储；系统备份和恢复由设备操作系统及其设置管理。

此帮助网站托管于 GitHub Pages，没有本站添加的分析、广告或登录。访问网页时，GitHub 可能处理常规请求信息，适用其隐私声明。商店、操作系统及你主动使用的 GitHub 支持渠道也适用各自政策。

支持问题请通过产品支持页提出。请勿提交密码、验证码、签名材料或私人计算记录。

WristCalc

Effective October 14, 2026

WristCalc is free, works offline and requires no account. The app has no ads, analytics SDK, developer backend or network permission. It does not transmit calculations to the developer.

Expressions and up to 20 successful results stay in the watch app's local storage for display, reuse and deletion. The app does not request location, contacts, health, camera or microphone access. There is no data-collection consent gate.

Delete records in Recent. Uninstalling removes the app's active local storage; operating-system backups and restoration follow your device's settings and policies.

This help site uses GitHub Pages, with no site-added analytics, ads or login. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under its privacy statement. Stores, operating systems and the GitHub support channel you choose to use have their own policies.

For support, use the product support page. Do not submit passwords, verification codes, signing material or private calculations.
